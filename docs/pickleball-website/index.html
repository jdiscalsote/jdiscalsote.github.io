How to Use Pickleball Scorer (Step-by-Step)

From opening setup to tournament certification — follow these 5 straightforward steps.

1
Configure Match Settings

Launch the app and tap New Match. Select Doubles or Singles, choose your scoring system (Traditional Side-Out or Rally Scoring), target score (11, 15, or 21), enter player names, and pick vibrant team colors.

💡 Tip: Set Win-by-2 to ON for official tournament matches
2
Observe Opening Serve (0-0-2)

The match launches on the Live Score screen. The header prominently displays the opening callout: 0 - 0 - 2. In pickleball, the opening serving team only gets one server before side-out.

⚡ Server #2 starts on the Right (Even) court
3
Record Points & Faults With Oversized Buttons

When the serving team wins the rally, tap the oversized green + POINT button. The app increments the score and automatically switches server sides. If the serving team faults, tap FAULT / SIDE OUT to rotate to Server 2 or transition serve to opponents.

🔄 Accidental tap? Tap the 1-tap Undo button
4
Call Timeouts & Track Side Switches

Tap the Timeout button to activate an official 60-second countdown with whistle vibration warnings. When a team reaches 6 or 8 points (or at each game finish), the app alerts players to swap sides of the court.

5
Finish Match & Export Scorecards

Once a team reaches the victory score, tap Conclude & Save Match. From the Match History log, you can view in-depth set analytics, or export a professional PDF Scorecard or CSV Data file to share with tournament directors.

Essential Knowledge

Official Pickleball Rules Cheat Sheet

Keep these four core rules handy during tournament matches and social play.

The "0-0-2" Opening Serve Rule

At the very beginning of every doubles match, the opening serving team is only allowed one server before a side-out is called. This server is designated as Server #2. This prevents the opening team from racking up an unfair lead before opponents have even served.

Even/Odd Service Positions

When a team's current score is an even number (0, 2, 4, 6...), the first server must serve from the Right court. When the score is an odd number (1, 3, 5...), the serve must originate from the Left court.

Non-Volley Zone (The Kitchen)

The 7-foot area on both sides of the net is the Non-Volley Zone. Players cannot volley the ball (strike it out of the air) while touching any portion of the kitchen or kitchen boundary lines. Players may only step into the kitchen if the ball has bounced in it first.

The Two-Bounce Rule

After the serve, each team must play their first shot off the bounce. That is, the receiving team must let the serve bounce, and the serving team must let the return of serve bounce before playing it. After two bounces have occurred, volleys are permitted!

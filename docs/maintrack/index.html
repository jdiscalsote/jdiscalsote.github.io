Never forget a service again.

Maintrack keeps a log of every oil change, PMS, filter and part on your vehicle, and reminds you before the next one is due. Free, offline, and your data never leaves your phone.

Download for Android See features

Android 7.0 or newer · Free · No account · No ads

Everything your vehicle logbook should be

Simple enough to use in the garage, thorough enough to keep a full history.

Every service in one log

Record the date, odometer, cost and notes for oil changes, gear oil, PMS, air filters, CVT cleaning and any part you add.

Know what’s due

See at a glance what is overdue, due soon or on track, with a progress bar for every part.

Reminders that work offline

Get a notification before a service is due and follow-ups if it’s overdue. Distance-based items use your own usage to estimate the date.

Scan your odometer

Point the camera at your gauge and the number is read on your phone. You check it before saving.

Receipt photos

Attach a photo of the receipt. The total is detected on your device, and you can correct it before saving.

More than one vehicle

Track a scooter, a motorcycle and a car in the same app, each with its own history and service intervals.

Back up and export

Export your records as JSON or CSV, and import a backup any time you switch phones.

Light, dark and full screen

Follows your phone’s theme, shows your currency, and can hide the system bars for a clean, app-like view.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

How it works

1

Add your vehicle

Enter its name and current odometer. Common services and their intervals are ready to use, and you can edit them.

2

Log what you did

After a service, add a record in a few taps. Scan the odometer or attach the receipt if you like.

3

Get reminded

Maintrack works out what’s due and notifies you before it is, so you stop guessing when you last changed the oil.

Your data stays on your phone

Maintrack was built to work without the internet, so there’s nothing to sign up for and nothing to send.

How to install

  1. Download the APK to your phone and open it.
  2. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.
  3. If Google Play Protect says it hasn’t seen an app from this developer before, tap Install anyway. Don’t tap OK, which cancels.
  4. Open Maintrack and add your first vehicle.

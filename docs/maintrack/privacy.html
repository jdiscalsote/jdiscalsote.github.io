Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Maintrack is a vehicle maintenance logbook that runs entirely on your phone. We don’t run servers for it, we don’t collect your data, and we can’t see what you record.

The short version

  • No account, sign-up or login.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • Everything you enter is stored only on your device.
  • We never receive, sell or share your information.

What the app stores on your device

Maintrack saves the information you enter so you can use it later: your vehicles (name, plate, odometer), service records (date, odometer, cost, notes), receipt photos you choose to attach, and your settings such as currency, units and reminder preferences. This data lives in the app’s private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera access only so you can photograph an odometer or a receipt. The text in those photos is read on your device using on-device text recognition. Photos taken only for scanning are processed and discarded. A receipt photo is kept only if you attach it to a record, and then it stays inside the app on your phone.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone based on the data you entered. No notification service or server is involved. You can turn reminders off at any time in the app’s Settings or in Android’s notification settings.

Internet access

Maintrack works without an internet connection and does not send your data over the network. The app can tell whether your phone is online or offline in order to show an indicator, but nothing is transmitted.

Backups and exports

You can export your records as a JSON or CSV file. Exporting opens Android’s share sheet, and the file goes only where you choose to send it. The app is set not to take part in Android’s automatic cloud backup, so your data is only ever backed up by you.

Deleting your data

Use Reset all data in the app’s Settings, or uninstall the app, and everything it stored is removed from your phone. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete.

Children

Maintrack is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. Because it collects no personal information, no data is gathered from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
